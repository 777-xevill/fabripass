# LoomPass — Buyer & Pilot Pack
## Every garment, a clearer story
Prepared 19 September 2026. Working SaaS prototype and proposed commercial offer.

## 1. Why LoomPass
Garment information often lives across spreadsheets, supplier emails and separate document folders. LoomPass puts product identity, material composition, evidence and traceability references into a consistent record so factories can prepare clearer buyer responses.

For management: visibility of completeness and open issues.
For merchandising: reusable style and batch information.
For compliance: supporting evidence linked to declarations.
For buyers: a structured product pack with visible information gaps.

## 2. What works today
- Individual signed-in workspaces with persistent product records.
- Style, batch, buyer, manufacturing origin, materials and care information.
- Four-stage supplier references and private evidence uploads.
- Internal review, expiry dates, eight-check readiness and change history.
- Private product passport previews and CSV/JSON exports.
- Shared sample tracking sessions, downloadable product QR codes and saved check-ins from another device.
- A buyer resource centre and saved pilot inquiries.

Team roles, shared factory tenants, subscription billing, ERP integrations, production identifiers, service commitments and regulatory registry integration remain production delivery scope. No customer results or certifications are claimed.

## 3. A garment scenario
Sample product: Everyday cotton tee / LP-TEE-001.
Style CT-2401. Batch BD-2609-01. Declared composition 100% cotton, 180 GSM.
Illustrative order: 2,400 pieces from Gazipur, Bangladesh.

Factory QC -> Packing -> Dispatch -> Buyer received.
Each confirmed demo event records product ID, checkpoint, server timestamp and event ID. All product claims are sample data.

A QR identifies the demo product record. It does not prove garment authenticity, physical possession, GPS location, shipment status or legal conformity. Check-ins are self-reported.

## 4. Two-device demonstration
1. Open the live demo on a laptop. Keep the session URL.
2. Select one of three sample garments.
3. Scan the QR using another phone's camera and open the link.
4. Choose a checkpoint and tap Confirm demo check-in.
5. Watch the laptop history update within five seconds.
6. Reload to verify persistence; export the session history as JSON.
7. Download the QR label to print or use in a meeting.

Opening a QR alone does not record an event; the visitor confirms the checkpoint. Copy the check-in link if scanning is unavailable. A new demo session preserves previous sessions and creates a separate history.

## 5. Six-week design-partner pilot
Proposed scope: one factory, 25 style/batch records, three suppliers and one participating buyer.

Week 1: Agree data fields, responsibilities, access and baseline three comparable buyer requests.
Week 2: Map source records and assign missing information.
Weeks 3-4: Train the working team and review five example records.
Week 5: Test the agreed buyer pack and capture feedback.
Week 6: Compare outcomes, document gaps and decide whether to continue.

Proposed acceptance measures: every scoped record accounted for; gaps assigned to owners; five reviewed examples; buyer feedback recorded. Target 90% agreed-field completeness and 30% lower median preparation time across comparable exercises. Targets depend on source data and participation and are not guaranteed.

## 7. Data and trust
Composition and origin are declarations requiring supporting evidence. Readiness measures internal data completeness and is not certification. Evidence review records an internal decision; it does not independently validate a document.

Anyone with the demo session link and Site access can view and add sample checkpoints. Do not add confidential or personal data. The app does not request GPS or camera access. Your phone camera resolves the QR URL.

Private factory APIs require sign-in and limit records to the authenticated user. Public demo endpoints do not expose factory records or evidence.

Pilot inquiries store supplied business contact details, company, role, message and submission time. Records are not publicly listed; no automatic email is sent. Retention, operating contact and formal processing terms must be agreed before commercial onboarding.

## 8. Production readiness and next step
Agree customer tenancy, least-privilege access, document handling, security review, backup and restore, retention, offboarding, stable identifiers, publication rules, support and buyer acceptance.

Confirm the product-specific legal requirements separately. LoomPass is not an EU conformity certificate and does not provide a registry integration.
